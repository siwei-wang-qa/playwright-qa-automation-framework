# Add Product to Cart

## User Story

As a logged-in customer,
I want to add a product to my shopping cart,
so that I can review it before checkout.

## Preconditions

- The customer is logged in with the framework's existing valid demo user.
- The shopping cart is initially empty.
- The customer is on the Products page.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The customer can add "Sauce Labs Backpack" to the shopping cart.
2. After adding the product, the shopping cart badge displays "1".
3. When the customer opens the shopping cart, "Sauce Labs Backpack"
   is displayed as a cart item.
4. The quantity of that cart item is "1".

## Scope

- Validate adding one product and viewing it in the cart.
- Checkout, payment, and removing products are outside this requirement.
